## bacteria.jpg

CDC PHIL 7136. Scanning electron micrograph of E. coli, source magnification 4890×. Credit CDC / Janice Haney Carr; content providers Evangeline Sowers and Janice Haney Carr. Public domain; attribution required by redistribution guidance on source page. https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/Details.aspx?pid=7136 . Original 5 µm scale bar retained. Source does not specify all preparation steps. Flagellar filaments are faint; outlines mark an example region, not a definitive segmentation.

## virus.jpg

CDC PHIL 8038. Transmission electron micrograph of influenza A (H5N1) particles. Credit CDC / Cynthia Goldsmith; Jackie Katz. Public domain; redistribution credits CDC and photographers. https://wwwn.cdc.gov/phil/Details.aspx?pid=8038 . No calibrated scale bar provided.
