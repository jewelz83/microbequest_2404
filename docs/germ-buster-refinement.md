# Germ Buster: Microbe Patrol refinement

Draft PR #3 only. Preserve the action-game choice, while removing medical weapon metaphors and fixed real-genus good/bad labels from the active experience. Pond and soil patrols use fictional ! targets and R residents. Symbols and colors explicitly communicate arcade mission roles, not species identity, biological harmfulness, treatment or health measurements. One fictional pulse tool replaces antibiotic/probiotic/sanitizer weapons. Full science notes and end-of-mission microbe/career links make the boundary visible.

Each mission has three waves, with four marked targets and two residents per wave. Targets crossing the right edge cost 15 arcade shield points; resident hits cost 20 safety points. Both meters start at 100. Stop targets for 10 arcade points; a resident hit subtracts 10, floored at zero. Survive all waves with both meters above zero to finish. Scores describe this arcade run, not scientific knowledge. Waves advance automatically after 1.5 seconds. No per-wave popup quiz, ammunition shop or mastery awards. The existing deterministic combat engine remains; optional role-specific balance effects let resident safety change on resident hits only. Legacy tests retain coverage of the prior tool contract.

Children choose a setting and steady/faster pace; faster respects the existing medium difficulty ceiling. Optional marker practice introduces the distinction before live play. Touch/mouse aim and pulse, a focused field supports arrow/Space controls, and large move/fire buttons provide alternatives. Optional auto pulse supports sustained action while requiring careful aiming around residents. Pause and restart confirmation freeze simulation and wave transition timers; cancellation preserves the mission. Leaving the document pauses the game. Auto pulse switches off on pause and at wave boundaries. Restart invalidates pending session callbacks. No global gameplay key capture.

The active route no longer renders legacy body-location claims, medical-tool panels, fake progress/mastery presentation, timed character popups, automatic score writes, analytics or the inline subscription upsell. Shared authentication/route/parent controls and billing logic remain unchanged. Legacy subcomponents remain unused for review/recovery; older browser fixtures predate this refinement and do not certify this active route. Mission state is memory-only.

Artwork is original code-native arcade graphics, explicitly not a microscopy simulation or to scale. The real-science handoff goes to Paramecium or Penicillium in the existing microscope lab. Sources already used by the world/lab: NIGMS What Is the Microbiome; OpenStax Biology 2e Groups of Protists; EPA Composting at Home. Linked in the game.

Physics/rule tests cover mixed waves, scaled aiming, target versus resident effects, three-wave objectives, success and loss. Hosted controls, runtime flow, responsive visuals and actual feel require browser checks beyond compilation. Family feedback, science/reading-age review, physical touch devices and broader accessibility testing remain before release. The moving field is not yet certified for nonvisual play.
